# Credits and licenses

All 100 songs in `songs/` are compositions that are in the public domain or released under an open license, and every MIDI file was taken from a source that states the file's license. Attribution for each file is listed below.

Sources:
- **Mutopia Project** (https://www.mutopiaproject.org) — public domain and Creative Commons sheet music typeset in LilyPond; the MIDI files are generated from those scores. Typesetters are credited per piece.
- **Wikimedia Commons** (https://commons.wikimedia.org) — MIDI files whose uploaders released them as public domain.
- **OpenGameArt.org** (https://opengameart.org) — contemporary music released under CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA or OGA-BY by their authors.
- **OpenEWLD** (https://github.com/00sapo/OpenEWLD) — public-domain lead sheets (melody and chord symbols) from the old Wikifonia archive, MIT-licensed dataset. The blues songs were rendered from these sheets by `tools/leadsheet2midi.py`, which adds a swung piano, bass and drum backing; that arrangement is released under CC0.
